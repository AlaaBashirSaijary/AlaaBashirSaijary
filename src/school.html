Why Education ERP

Built for how schools really work

Attendance, fees and parent communication are the daily pain points. This system puts them in one place instead of paper registers, spreadsheets and phone calls.

Arabic first

Right-to-left Arabic by default with a one-click switch to English. The whole interface and the messages to parents are built for Arabic-speaking schools.

One system, four roles

Admin, teacher, accountant and parent each see only what they need. Parents see only their own children.

Parents stay informed

Attendance, report cards, fee reminders and announcements reach parents by WhatsApp or SMS, with a full log of every message.

Your own copy

Each school runs a separate copy with its own database, on the hosting it chooses (Docker, Render or a VPS).

Features

Everything the school office needs

QR attendance

Scan student QR cards at the gate with a barcode scanner or the camera, or take attendance for a whole class manually. Daily attendance report with CSV export.

Grades & report cards

Create exams, record marks, print the student certificate and send the report card to parents.

Fees & payments

Installment plans per student, payments with printable receipts, an overdue list and fee reminders, plus a financial report with CSV export.

Parent notifications

WhatsApp or SMS messages sent through a queue, announcements to parents and a log of all messages. Real sending needs your messaging provider keys.

Timetable

Build class timetables with built-in protection against scheduling clashes.

Staff attendance

A daily register for teachers and staff (present, late, absent, leave) with check-in and check-out, and a monthly report with attendance rate.

Academic years

Create a year with its terms, set the current year, promote students to the next class or graduate them, and switch the year across every page.

Users & access

Admins create teacher and accountant accounts. Parents register themselves by matching the student number and the phone registered at the school.

Installable mobile app

Install it on Android, iPhone, iPad or desktop with its own icon and window. It needs HTTPS, and data entry needs an internet connection.

FAQ

Questions school owners ask

What is the Education ERP System?

It is a school management web app built with Laravel 11 and Livewire. It covers attendance, grades, fees, parent notifications, timetables, staff attendance and academic years, in Arabic and English.

Can I try it before buying?

Yes. The interactive demo runs in your browser and lets you sign in as every role. It is simulated, so nothing is saved and no messages are sent.

How do parents receive notifications?

Messages are queued and sent by WhatsApp or SMS through your provider. You set the channel and provider keys in the configuration; until then messages are only logged.

Do parents have their own accounts?

Yes. Parents register themselves by entering the student number and the phone registered at the school, and they see only their own children.

Does it work on phones?

Yes. It is installable as a mobile app (PWA) on Android, iPhone and iPad. Installing needs HTTPS, and entering data needs an internet connection.

What do I need to run it for my school?

A server or hosting that runs PHP, chosen and paid for by the school: I do not provide hosting. The system ships with Docker files, a Render blueprint and a VPS setup with automatic HTTPS. Each school gets a separate copy and database.

Which languages does it support?

Arabic (right-to-left) by default and English, with a switch in the interface.

How much does it cost?

The price depends on your school. Message me on WhatsApp or by email with your needs and I will send a quote.
