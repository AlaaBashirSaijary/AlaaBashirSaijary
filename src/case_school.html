Education ERP: one system for a school’s daily work

A school management system I designed and built, in Arabic first, with four roles and its own copy for every school.

The brief

What the system had to do

Schools handle attendance, fees and parent communication every day, often with paper registers, spreadsheets and phone calls. The goal was to bring these into one place that office staff, teachers, accountants and parents can all use, in Arabic.

Key decisions

Four choices that shaped the system

01

Arabic first, not translated later

The interface is right-to-left by default, and so are the messages sent to parents, with a one-click switch to English.

02

Each person sees only what they need

Admin, teacher, accountant and parent have separate workspaces. A parent sees only their own children, and registers by matching the student number and the phone the school holds.

03

A separate copy for every school

Every school gets its own copy with its own database, on the hosting it chooses. The school keeps its data, and I never run a shared service that holds it.

04

Messages through a queue, with a log

WhatsApp and SMS messages to parents are queued and every one is logged, so the school can see what was sent. Real sending needs the school’s own provider keys.

What it does

What is in the system

  • QR attendance at the gate, or manual class attendance, with a daily report and CSV export
  • Exams, marks, printable certificates and report cards sent to parents
  • Installment plans, receipts, an overdue list and a financial report
  • Timetables with protection against clashes, and a staff attendance register
  • Academic years: terms, promotion to the next class and graduation
