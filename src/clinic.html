FAQ

Questions doctors ask about Aayadati

What is Aayadati?

Aayadati (Clinic Manager) is a clinic management app for doctors. It keeps patient records, appointments, prescriptions, medical reports and financial reports in one place, with a full Arabic interface.

Does it need an internet connection?

No. Patient records, appointments, prescriptions and reports all work offline. Internet is only used for the optional AI assistant and for checking whether a new version is available.

Which devices does it run on?

It is built with Flutter and is designed for iPad and Android devices.

Is it a subscription?

No. It is a one-time payment with no monthly or yearly fees. The price is agreed after a live demo.

Is there a free trial?

Yes. You can try every feature free for 14 days before you buy.

Where is my patient data stored?

On your own device in a local database. You can choose where the database is stored, for example on an external drive. Nothing is sent to a server.

Can several devices share the same data?

Not at the moment. The app works on a single device and stores data locally, so it is best for one doctor or one clinic workstation.

Which medical specialties is it for?

Any specialty. The examination form is fully customizable, so each clinic adds the fields it needs.
