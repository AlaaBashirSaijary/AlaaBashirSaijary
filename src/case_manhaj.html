The brief

What the app had to be

One app for the three things people open every day: the Quran, prayer times and azkar. It should be quiet and fast, and it should keep working with no internet connection.

Key decisions

Four choices that shaped the app

01

Everything works offline

All 604 Mushaf pages and five tafsirs are in the app, so reading never depends on a connection.

02

No accounts, no ads, no tracking

The app asks for no sign-up and carries no ads or trackers, which keeps it quiet and keeps the user’s data on their device.

03

Prayer times that fit every place

16 calculation methods, adhan alerts, a Qibla compass and a home-screen widget, so people can choose the method their community follows.

04

Accessible and multilingual

An Arabic and English interface, 10 languages for translated meanings and screen-reader support.

What it does

What is in the app

  • The full Madinah Mushaf, with 6,236 tappable verses
  • Prayer times, adhan, Qibla and a home-screen widget
  • 1,896 hadiths from Riyad as-Salihin
  • Azkar and a tasbeeh counter
