The brief

What the app had to do

A doctor who still keeps a paper notebook or a spreadsheet needs patient records, appointments, prescriptions and money in one place, in Arabic, without depending on a server or a connection during a full workday.

Key decisions

Five choices that shaped the app

01

Offline, with the data on the doctor’s own device

All clinic data lives in a local database. There is no server and no downtime, and the doctor can choose where the database is stored, for example on an external drive.

02

An exam form that adapts to any specialty

Instead of fixed fields, the examination form is fully customizable, so an eye clinic and a dental clinic can each add exactly what they record.

03

Arabic all the way to the printed page

The interface is right-to-left, and prescriptions, reports and patient files print as Arabic PDFs with the clinic’s own letterhead.

04

Safety nets around the data

Because the data is local, backups matter: manual backup with periodic reminders, an automatic safety copy before every update, an optional PIN lock, roles and an activity log.

05

AI only when the doctor asks, and always reviewed

The optional assistant suggests dosages, drafts reports and answers questions about the clinic’s numbers. It needs internet only when used, and the doctor reviews everything before saving or printing.

Trade-offs

What I chose not to build, and why

Keeping data on one device means the app does not sync between devices today, so it suits one doctor or one clinic workstation. I accepted that on purpose: it keeps the data private and the app fast, and it is a limit the product page states openly.

What it does

What is in the app

  • Patient records with instant search, history, allergies, PDF export and Excel or CSV import
  • Appointments with double-booking protection, a waiting-room screen and a WhatsApp booking link
  • Prescriptions, sick-leave notes and medical reports with an archive for instant reprints
  • Follow-up lists with one-tap call and a ready WhatsApp reminder
  • Financial reports: fees, amount collected and outstanding balance
